The rules that keep the books boring.

Angular checks the form. The database checks the truth. A sent invoice is a snapshot, not a live reference.

01

One money trail

Unbilled time and expenses are copied onto the invoice. Change a rate later and old lines stay put.

02

Integers only

Minor units plus an ISO currency. No floats in the form, the API, or the database.

03

Void or credit

Untouched invoices can be voided. Anything paid gets a credit note. Numbers are never reused.

04

A door, not an account

Clients open a token link. They see their invoices, the PDF, and how to pay. Nothing else.

Balance

Total − payments − credit notes.

Four moves. Then it is filed.

Capture, bill, collect, correct. No CRM, no payroll, no inventory.

01

Capture

One running timer. Manual entries accept 1h30 or 90m. Receipts stay in private storage.

02

Bill

Pull the unbilled queue. Include, exclude, or write off. Send freezes the lines and the PDF.

03

Collect

Record a payment with a reference. Partials are normal. Overpay becomes client credit.

04

Correct

Reverse a payment with a new row. Issue a credit note. The original document stays.
