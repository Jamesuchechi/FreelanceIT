ARCHITECTURAL INTEGRITY

The four rules that keep the books boring.

Angular validates the user interface for instant responsiveness. Supabase and PostgreSQL enforce the mathematical truth. A sent invoice is a frozen snapshot, never a live reference.

01

One Money Trail

Unbilled time and expense rows are copied onto the invoice when sent. If you raise your rate next month, historical invoices and past lines remain unaltered.

time_entries → invoice_lines (frozen)
02

Integers Only

Every monetary value is stored in minor units (cents, kobo, pence) with an ISO 4217 currency code. No floating-point division drift anywhere in the DB or API.

amount_minor: 1500000, cur: 'NGN'
03

Void or Credit Only

Untouched invoices can be voided, releasing time entries back to draft. Any invoice with recorded payments is corrected with a formal Credit Note. Invoices are never deleted.

CN-2026-0001 (issued) · No deletes
04

A Door, Not an Account

Clients access their invoices through unguessable cryptographic token links. They see the live status, bank instructions, and download the PDF. No client sign-up friction.

/p/tok_94f2a781... · 0 Passwords
